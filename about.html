Our Story

DA-Threads was born from a passion for quality and style. What started as a small venture has grown into Kenya's premier t-shirt brand, known for exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail.

We believe that everyone deserves to wear comfortable, stylish clothing that makes them feel confident. Our mission is to provide premium quality t-shirts that are accessible to everyone, while supporting local artisans and sustainable practices.

Our Values

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices and eco-friendly materials in all our products.

Quality

Premium materials and expert craftsmanship ensure every t-shirt meets our high standards.

Community

We support local communities and empower local artisans through fair trade practices.

Innovation

We continuously innovate in design and production to bring you the latest trends.
