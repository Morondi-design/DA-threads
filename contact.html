Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We offer free shipping on orders above KES 2,500. Standard delivery takes 2-5 business days within Nairobi.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused items in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days.

Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes, we offer special discounts for bulk orders. Please contact us at info@dathreads.com for more information.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers for all purchases.
